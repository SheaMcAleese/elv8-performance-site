FOR ORGANISATIONS

Practical AI for teams who have real work to do.

Your people are already using AI. Some use it well, most use it quietly, and a few use it in ways that should worry you. The gap is rarely the tool. It is knowing which job to hand it, how to brief it, and how to check what comes back.

The three ways to work together

  1. AI Working Session

    $4,500 (sports bodies, schools and not-for-profits $3,000)

    • A 7-question form before we meet, about 15 minutes per person.
    • A 20-minute call to shape the day.
    • Three hours, hands on, with 6 to 10 leaders. We find the real problems, practise briefing AI on your own work, and decide which jobs AI should take on, help with, or stay away from.
    • You leave with one or two pilots, each with an owner, a check, stop rules and a measure.
    • A 30-day check-in, booked before you leave the room.

  2. AI Capability Programme

    $16,000 (sports bodies, schools and not-for-profits $12,000)

    • For up to 15 staff, four sessions over six weeks.
    • Everyone picks one real workflow from their own job and builds it between sessions.
    • Your team leaves with a shared playbook: what works, what to check, what not to hand over.
    • A 30-day review.

  3. AI Build Sprint

    $18,000

    • Four weeks. One working AI tool or workflow, built with your team, not for it.
    • Designed around a job you already do, tested on real work, then handed over with training and a plain-English guide.

How it works

  1. A 20-minute call. What is the job, who is in the room, and what would make it worth it?

  2. A short proposal within two working days. Scope, dates, price. No surprises.

  3. The work. In person in Hawke's Bay or anywhere in New Zealand, or online. Travel outside Hawke's Bay is charged at cost and agreed in the proposal.

  4. A 30-day check-in. Did it stick? What next?

What I won't do

  • Promise savings before we have measured anything.
  • Sell you software.
  • Leave you with a tool nobody owns.
