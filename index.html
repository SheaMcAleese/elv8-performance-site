Shea McAleese

Four Olympic Games and 320 caps for New Zealand. Left home at 22 to play in Germany, coached in Belgium, and now coaches high performance hockey.

AI turned out to be the same question. In 2026 he completed MIT Sloan Executive Education's Leading the AI-Driven Organization. He builds the systems he teaches, so he knows where they break.
