The promise

What you can do at the end. Not what you will know.

  • Turn rough notes into a structured session plan in minutes.
  • Generate personalised athlete feedback that sounds like you, not like a robot.
  • Build a season and periodisation overview in a spreadsheet, without being a spreadsheet person.
  • Turn a training or a game into a clean report or deck.
  • Interrogate your own coaching notes and documents, and get straight answers back.

  • Walk away with your own working setup. Not a folder of theory.

The test of every session is the same: you leave able to do something new with your real work.

The six weeks

One live session a week. One real task between them.

Sixty to seventy five minutes, hands on, working on your coaching rather than on slides. Every week is applied to live work only, never to practice mode.

  1. Week one. Setup, and how to actually talk to AI

    The frame that makes the difference between a vague answer and a usable one. First win on a real task you bring to the room.

  2. Week two. Session planning and periodisation

    Build plans fast. Get the season on one page. Spreadsheets, without becoming a spreadsheet person.

  3. Week three. Athlete feedback and comms, in your voice

    A feedback engine tuned to how you speak, so what goes out still sounds like it came from you.

  4. Week four. Your second brain

    Get your own coaching knowledge organised and searchable, then ask it hard questions and get real answers.

  5. Week five. Reports, decks and analysis

    Turn a training block or a game into something professional enough to put in front of a board or a parent.

  6. Week six. Make it stick

    Your personal setup, and the habits that keep it running when the season gets loud. What comes next.

Before you ask

The four things everybody says.

I am not techy.

That is exactly who this is for. We build it together in the session. You talk, it types. There is no code in this anywhere, and there never will be. If you can write a text message you have the technical skill required.

I do not have time.

This gives time back. If it does not, the guarantee is right there. The commitment is seventy five minutes a week plus one task applied to work you were already going to do.

AI feels impersonal. That is not coaching.

Agreed, and that is why the feedback week exists. It drafts, you stay the coach. Done properly it sharpens your voice and your judgment rather than replacing them. Done badly it sounds like a robot, which is precisely the failure we spend a week designing out.

I could learn this free on YouTube.

You could. You have not. That is not a dig, it is the point. This is done with you, tailored to your coaching, with someone checking the work each week. That is the difference between watching and doing, and it is the only thing you are actually paying for.
